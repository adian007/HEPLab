Browse examples from three evidence tiers. Open a card to inspect its source, result identity, model scope, and limitations.

<Aside type="caution" title="Example evidence scope">
  Synthetic examples are not CMS data. A reference-table reproduction covers the named table and interpolation domain. The recorded CMS resonance example has a failed held-out Υ(2S) gate and is not detector calibration or independent validation. HEPLab does not upload or publish your local runs.
</Aside>
