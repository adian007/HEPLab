Magnetic curvature and sagitta sweep

Synthetic equation/model comparison; no measured tracks. Muons start at the origin along +x, with η = 0, and are propagated for 1 m in vacuum. The exact uniform-field rows span momentum, charge, and field strength. The separate OAE rows use the pinned tracker field function at nominal 3.8 T.

Exact uniform-field sweep

The signed sagitta is the transverse midpoint's signed distance from the endpoint chord. Radius is pT / (0.299792458 |q/e| |B|); zero field is straight.
