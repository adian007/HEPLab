PDG 2025 reference-table reproduction

Mean muon energy loss in silicon

Reference-table reproduction, not a detector measurement. This example reproduces PDG 2025 mean muon ionization stopping power in silicon. The held-out maximum absolute relative error is 0.4105% against a predeclared 2% threshold. Radiative loss, event-by-event straggling, sensor response, and CMS calibration are not tested.

Declared interpolation result

Fit scope: log-linear interpolation in kinetic energy using 25 alternating training rows; 24 rows are held out from the fit. Valid tabulated domain: 0.1 to 100 GeV kinetic energy. Requests outside that range are rejected.

Target quantity: mean ionization stopping power, GeV/m. It is not a stochastic single-particle loss distribution.
