Follow a declared muon pair

These are model paths and predicted guide-surface crossings for a declared back-to-back test pair. They are not CMS events, detector hits, or measured trajectories. The run uses a continuous PDG-table mean loss, a Gaussian Highland scattering core, and separate illustrative electronics.

Expected field result

For each 5 GeV/c transverse muon in a uniform 3.8 T ideal field, the analytic radius is pT/(0.299792458 |q| B) = 4.389001 m. The run applies the field over its declared 1 m segment, then uses one illustrative 1 mm silicon slab.
